# AGENTS.md — Budget Inspector Agent Guidelines & Evidence Rules

This document governs the behavior, evidence standards, and journalistic ethics of AI coding agents interacting with the **Budget Inspector** repository.

---

## 1. CORE RESPONSIBILITIES & BOUNDARIES

1. **Evidence-First Analysis**: Always ground findings in exact, reproducible SQL queries executed on normalized DuckDB tables derived from official Department of Budget and Management (DBM) General Appropriations Act (GAA) spreadsheets.
2. **Provenance Mandatory**: Every number, delta, or lead reported MUST include source provenance down to the specific source file, sheet, and row numbers.
3. **Deterministic Arithmetic**: Language models MUST NOT calculate or summarize budget numbers in prose. SQL/Python engine handles all mathematical operations, sums, deltas, and ratios. The AI interprets and explains the deterministic results.
4. **Appropriation vs. Expenditure**:
   - **Appropriation**: A legislative authorization to incur obligations for specified purposes.
   - **Allotment**: An authorization issued by DBM to incur obligations.
   - **Obligation**: A commitment by a government agency (e.g., contract signed).
   - **Disbursement**: Actual payment/cash outflow.
   - *Agents must NEVER treat an appropriation as an actual expenditure, completed project, or paid award.*

---

## 2. JOURNALISTIC & ETHICAL LANGUAGE RULES

To maintain objectivity and credibility, agents must adhere to strict terminological boundaries.

### ALLOWED TERMS
- *unusual change*
- *large variance*
- *anomaly* / *statistical outlier*
- *investigative lead*
- *item worth reviewing*
- *budget concentration*
- *reclassification candidate*
- *possible rename*
- *structural shift*

### FORBIDDEN LABELS (Unless supported by separate external authoritative judicial or official audit findings)
- ❌ *corrupt* / *corruption*
- ❌ *fraudulent* / *fraud*
- ❌ *pork barrel*
- ❌ *ghost project*
- ❌ *kickback*
- ❌ *illegal* / *criminal*

---

## 3. LEAD GENERATION PRINCIPLES

- A lead generated by Budget Inspector is a **question**, not a **conclusion**.
- Example Good Lead Statement:
  > *"DPWH Program X increased by ₱12.4B (145%) from FY 2025 to FY 2026. The next step is to verify whether this reflects program expansion, sub-program consolidation, or a newly created sub-item."*
- Confidence ratings (`HIGH`, `MEDIUM`, `LOW`) refer strictly to **confidence in the underlying data relationship**, NEVER to confidence that wrongdoing occurred.

---

## 4. AGENTIC WORKFLOW STANDARD

When answering user queries or performing automated budget investigations, agents must follow this 10-step workflow:

1. **Classify Intent**: Identify question type (lookup, top increases, new items, reallocations, flood control, etc.).
2. **Inspect Schema**: Verify field names and UACS code structures for FY 2025 and FY 2026.
3. **Execute Deterministic Query**: Run DuckDB SQL via `src/budget_inspector/queries.py`.
4. **Validate Arithmetic**: Verify row sums against official DBM agency totals.
5. **Entity Resolution**: Apply exact, structured PAP, or conservative fuzzy matching rules.
6. **Retrieve Provenance**: Extract source file, sheet, row indices for both comparison years.
7. **Calculate Metrics**: Compute absolute change, percentage change (handling zero baselines with `NEW_IN_2026`), and share shifts.
8. **Generate Research Receipt**: Save machine-readable JSON receipt to `queries/investigations/`.
9. **Formulate Conservative Explanation**: Describe findings using approved neutral terminology.
10. **Include Standard Caveat**: Explicitly note that appropriations do not equal released or spent funds.
